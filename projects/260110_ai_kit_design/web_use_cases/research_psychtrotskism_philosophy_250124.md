## User Patterns

### How questions were asked
- **Socratic + collaborative building**: User didn't just ask questions — they contributed original insights and expected me to build on them, not just agree
- **Corrective refinement**: Frequently corrected my formulations to be more precise ("В1 никогда не сработает", "Это как раз ловушка")
- **Meta-aware**: Explicitly noted when I made psychtrotskist moves myself ("ты предлагаешь фокус на просветительстве?" — catching me proposing strategy after reading 2 texts)
- **Interruption tolerance**: Apologized for interrupting my response, then asked me to restart — respectful of process

### What worked well
- **User's own metaphors**: The "crash test" metaphor for quantum measurement was powerful — I should have highlighted it more
- **Table format**: Scientific parallels table was well-received, requested for final output
- **Acknowledging corrections**: When user corrected me, building on their correction rather than defending
- **Practical output**: Creating the actual MD file moved from abstract to concrete
- **Humility about limitations**: Admitting "я не знаю" and "это был бы психтроцкизм с моей стороны" landed well

### What didn't work
- **Premature strategizing**: I proposed "third path" for KOB strategy — user correctly identified this as overreach
- **Answering adjacent question**: User asked "что должно произойти" — I answered with scenarios but didn't directly address the question until corrected
- **Over-elaboration**: Some responses could have been shorter; user's own formulations were often more precise than mine
- **Interrupted mid-thought**: First attempt at synthesis was cut off — could have been more modular
